事件摘要

核心突破：识别并修复标准softmax/log-softmax/交叉熵实现中的Softmax Collapse与Softmax Cancellation两个数值缺陷，消除Slingshot现象并改善grokking、蒸馏及训练稳定性。 解决的问题：标准softmax和交叉熵实现存在两个数值bug：目标logit远大于其他logit时梯度被舍入为零（Softmax Collapse），以及学生与教师logit相等时仍产生非零梯度（Softmax Cancellation），导致训练不稳定和蒸馏漂移。 研究价值：为深度学习训练基础设施提供了底层数值稳定性修复，影响grokking速度、精度保持和知识蒸馏可靠性，对大规模模型训练具有普遍适用性。

A1A Fix for Softmax and Cross-Entropy

Standard implementations of softmax, log-softmax, and cross-entropy have two numerical bugs. The first bug, Softmax Collapse, rounds the target logit's gradient to zero if that one logit is much larger than the others, resulting in gradients that no longer sum to zero. The second bug, Softmax Cancellation, occurs when student logits are close to teacher logits, and produces non-zero gradients even when the student and teacher logits are equal.
